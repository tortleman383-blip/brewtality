# Changes

What's new in Brewtality, newest first.

Brewtality also has a Roblox version that copies this game exactly, and it reads this file to know what to copy. So every change to how the game plays, looks or sounds gets an entry here, in the same commit as the change. Write each entry like this:

```
### 2026-10-27 · Spitters aim ahead of you
- Spitter goo now leads a moving target instead of aiming where you stood.
- Numbers: goo speed 9 → 11; lead 0 → 0.35 s.
- Code: launchGoo, TYPES.spitter.
```

- **The first lines** are for players: what changed, in plain words.
- **Numbers** gives the old and new values, so they can be copied straight across.
- **Code** names the functions, tables or sections touched, so the change can be found without rereading the whole file.
- **Web only:** mark changes that only matter here, such as PeerJS co-op, the claude.ai page, the downloaded file, the test lab console, or this repo's tools. Write `Web only:` and the reason, so the Roblox side knows to skip it.
- **Fixes and refactors:** a bug fix is an entry too. A refactor with no visible effect gets one line.

### 2026-10-27 · Brewkout and Slime Snake (arcade patch 2), and rooms in the test lab
- **Brewkout** is plugged in on the arcade's second cabinet: breakout with a ladle for a paddle and a potion for a ball. Six rows of ingredient-coloured bricks; clear the wall and a new one comes in a level up, faster. Move with A and D or the arrow keys, or the mouse (or drag a finger); space, click or tap launches. You get 3 balls. Some bricks glint: break one and an ingredient capsule falls, and catching it on the ladle does what that ingredient does in a brew, more or less: **doom** makes a fireball that goes straight through bricks, **frost** slows the ball, **feather** widens the ladle, **glowcap** splits every ball in three.
- **Slime Snake** is plugged in on the third: a slime that grows as it eats ingredients (doom caps, glowcaps, feathers, eyes, frost) and gets faster with every snack. Every fourth snack a spike patch comes up somewhere, never right in front of you. Hitting a wall, a spike or yourself ends it. Steer with WASD or the arrow keys, or swipe on the screen.
- Both work like Dragon Flap: 1 coin, 3 plays a visit, a tune of their own while you play, and walking away mid-game pauses it (PAUSED · TAP TO CARRY ON). Their attract screens say how to play. Goblin Invaders is still OUT OF ORDER.
- **The cabinet screens use a pixel font now** (VT323). It was meant to from the start; the game never loaded it, so Dragon Flap's text had been falling back to a plain monospace.
- **A gamepad's stick or d-pad works as the arrow keys** at a machine (steers the snake, moves the ladle; up also flaps).
- Numbers: Brewkout pays 1 / 2 / 4 / 7 / 10 tickets for 0–9 / 10–24 / 25–47 / 48–79 / 80+ bricks; wall 8 × 6 bricks of 22 × 9 px; 14% of bricks hold a capsule; ball speed 105 + 14 × level + 0.6 × bricks (that last part at most 40) px/s, × 0.6 in slow-mo; ladle 34 px wide (52 when widened), keys move it 190 px/s; capsules fall 55 px/s; fireball 6 s, slow-mo 7 s, wide ladle 9 s, split adds 2 balls per ball (at most 6 at once); where the ball lands on the ladle sets its angle (up to 1.05 rad). Slime Snake pays 1 / 2 / 4 / 7 / 10 tickets for 0–4 / 5–11 / 12–19 / 20–29 / 30+ snacks; grid 16 × 19 cells of 12 px; starts 3 long, one step per 0.16 s, × 0.955 per snack down to 0.075 s; up to 2 turns queue between steps; a spike every 4 snacks, at least 5 cells from the head. Tunes: Brewkout 132 bpm, Slime Snake 120 bpm. A visit is now 15 plays (was 9): 3 each on Dragon Flap, Brewkout, Slime Snake, skee-ball and cups.
- Code: arcade module: makeCab (the shared cabinet frame: coin, end, step, key, pointer, leave, reset), overlays (attract, closed, broke, paused and game-over cards), brewkout, snake, TUNES (was TUNE, one per game); cabinet() takes its game from [flap, brewkout, snake] and the spot carries it as S.game; update, afterRender, enter, leave, endNow, key and pointer go through S.game instead of flap; afterRender keeps the inverse of the screen fit (play.inv) so taps and drags land in the game's own pixels; #arcScreen gets pointermove/up; flap gets id 'flap'; ARC_PLAYS adds brewkout and snake; pollPad sends the stick and d-pad to the arcade as arrow keys; a window blur lets go of held cabinet keys; the Google Fonts link adds VT323 and the arcade asks for it to load.
- Web only: **the test lab can summon rooms.** Its console has ROOM and SUMMON ROOM: a door opens in the chamber's back wall onto a freshly built room of that kind (Goblin Arcade, secret shop, gift, hoard, treasure room, garden, a plain fight, pitfalls, spike floor, orb turrets, any boss, or the goblin shop), on the console's FLOOR. You land just inside its door, and can walk back to the chamber through it or use BACK TO CHAMBER; the subject comes back when you do. Fights and bosses play out like a run (doors lock, the boss's shop caves in after), and a boss room's stairs lead back to the chamber. +20 COINS · +20 TICKETS tops up the purse, which the console now shows. In the lab a knockback can't carry you through an open door. Code: labRooms, labMakeRoom, labGo, inChamber (the chamber-only lab checks in the Lurker's field and the Veiled Queen's veils now use it, so a summoned boss acts as it would in a run), labItems, labBody, labHud, labTick, labSpawn, enterLab, leaveLab, nextFloor, doorClamp, the .arcading CSS hides #labHud.

### 2026-10-27 · The Goblin Arcade (patch 1 of the den rebuild)
- **The gambling den is now the Goblin Arcade**, still one of the secret rooms behind the X brick. Dark plum stone, a loud carpet, pink and cyan neon strips instead of torches, and a GOBLIN ARCADE sign over the counter. The goblin bookie is replaced by **a garden gnome in shades** (pointy red hat with a flop, big beard, gold chain and medal) on a stool behind a wider counter, with a wall of prizes behind him. The rubble from the shop wall is gone in this room.
- **Machines cost coins and pay tickets.** Tickets show under your coins while you're in the arcade, and belong to the run like coins do. Each machine has 3 plays per visit, then he closes it.
- **Walk up to a machine and hold E (or PLAY on touch)** to step up to it. The camera pushes in (0.55 s, the view narrows to 50° at a cabinet, 58° at skee-ball, cups and the counter). Esc or Backspace, STEP BACK on touch, or B or Start on a gamepad steps you back. Mouse look, slinging, bonking and the inventory are off while you're at a machine. The mouse is let go so you can point; after stepping back a click takes it again.
- **Dragon Flap** (cabinet 1 of 4): a Flappy-style game with a baby dragon through dungeon drain pipes, on a vertical monitor. Space, W, Up, Enter, click or tap to flap. Once you're nose to the glass, the 3D room stops drawing and the game runs on a flat layer fitted exactly over the screen (so it's smooth on slow machines). Walk away mid-run and it pauses; a flap carries on. It has its own little tune. The other three cabinets (Brewkout, Slime Snake, Goblin Invaders) are there with OUT OF ORDER on their screens; they come in later patches.
- **Skee-ball** replaces the pot toss. Nine balls a game. Hold to wind up (space, or hold the mouse or a finger) and let go to roll; aim with the mouse or A and D. Power rises for 0.8 s then falls if you keep holding. The board scores 10 / 20 / 30 / 50 from the outside in, and 100 in the two top corners.
- **Cups** stay, on the counter, with the gnome shuffling. From your second round he sometimes palms the coin: his medal flashes and he glances away. Call him out with the HE CHEATED! button or C. Click a cup or press 1, 2 or 3 to pick.
- **The prize counter**: step up to the ticket dispenser at the other end of the counter. Ingredient (a random one) 10 tickets, 5 coins 25, Mystery item (one from the shop list) 60, **Paper Crown** 100. Keys 1–4 buy. The prize flies off the shelf at you.
- **Paper Crown**: a new brewer outfit, ARCADE KING, that only exists once you've bought it at the counter. It's saved between runs; BREWER → OUTFIT skips it until then.
- Numbers: plays per machine per visit 3 (was cups 3, toss 5). Dragon Flap: 1 coin; pays 1 / 2 / 4 / 7 / 10 tickets for 0–2 / 3–7 / 8–14 / 15–24 / 25+ gates; gravity 620 px/s², flap −205 px/s, gap 84 → 62 px (−1.2 per gate), speed 72 → 118 px/s (+2 per gate), pipe every 92 px. Cups: 2 coins (same), right cup 5 tickets (was 6 coins), catching him cheat 10 tickets, 7 + round + floor swaps (max +6), swap time 0.32 − 0.012 × (round + floor) s (min 0.2), cheats 30% of rounds from round 2. Prizes: 10 / 25 / 60 / 100 tickets. A visit's best case is about 90 tickets for 9 coins, roughly 18 coins' worth back (the old den paid about 40 coins).

### 2026-10-26 · The Big Baby, turret orbs, and the guide in the pause menu
- **The Cyclops Chief is now the Big Baby**: a giant cyclops baby in a blue bonnet, a nappy and booties, with a dummy in its mouth and a rattle for a club. Same stairs-guarding spot from floor 1, same health.
- **It charges from floor 1** (was floor 3). A red lane lights up, then it runs. If it catches you it stops there. If it misses, it runs into the wall, sits down hard on its bottom and bawls for 3 s (was 1.8 s dazed): legs out, rubbing its eye, and that's your chance. The crash brings rocks down from the ceiling.
- **Tantrums replace its ground slam**: it spits its dummy out, wails, and stomps 4 times (6 once enraged). Every stomp hurts anyone inside the red ring round it and shakes rocks loose. It can throw one from across the room too, not just up close.
- **Falling rocks**: a red outline on the floor for half a second, then a rock drops out of the ceiling there (1 heart if you're under it) and crumbles away. Every other rock lands near a player; the rest land anywhere. Dust rains from the ceiling when it happens.
- Moves by floor: charge 1, rock toss 2, rage 3 (was 2), aftershocks 4 (now after a tantrum). First meeting says MIND THE CEILING. The Cyclops King is unchanged.
- **Turrets fire slow glowing red orbs** instead of dark darts: much easier to see and step aside from. Still aimed where you're standing, still hit creatures too.
- **COMBOS is gone from the title menu** (the guide book has a combos page). **The pause menu's COMBOS button is now GUIDE**, opening the guide book mid-run.

### 2026-10-26 · Charged bonks, contact damage, spikes, and rancid on pickup
- **The bonk charges.** Tap for a jab: 1 damage, and it no longer dazes or shoves, so it can't interrupt an attack. Hold 1 s for a heavy bonk: 2 damage, dazes and shoves (bosses take the damage but aren't interrupted). Hold 2 s for a BIG BONK: 4 damage, a big shove that can slam them into a wall, and it staggers bosses too. You walk at 55% speed while winding up; the crosshair ring fills white, then orange at heavy, then flashes red at full; the ladle hauls back over your shoulder and trembles when it's full. A click sounds at heavy and a ding at full. The "every third bonk in a row is a BIG BONK" rule is gone.
- **Walking into a creature hurts** (1 heart, and you bounce off). Dazed, frozen and floating creatures are safe to touch. Enemies walking up to you don't count, only you walking into them.
- **Spiky creatures:** the Stumpling (pale thorns round its back and sides) and the Tin Knight (spiked pauldrons and a spike on its shield). Bonking one with anything less than a full charge pricks you for 1 heart. The first prick in a run shows a tip.
- **Spoilage is a roll, not a timer.** Doom shrooms, glowcaps, frost and cyclops eyes picked up in the dungeon (scraped, or dropped by a creature) come up rancid 1 time in 5. Nothing goes off while you carry it any more. Feathers, bog water, horns, iron, shop buys, fountain water and your starting shelf are always fresh. The pot screen's freshness bars are gone.
- **Preserving Salt** halves the rancid chance (was: ingredients keep twice as long). **Fermenting Jar** doubles it and still makes rancid extras hit everyone. With both, it's back to 1 in 5.
- **The chalked controls on the first room's floor are gone** (the guide book covers them). Floor decor in that room is no longer kept clear of where the chalk was.
- The guide's controls, scraping, Stumpling and Tin Knight pages, its SPIKY tag, the shop tags for the salt and jar, and the out-of-brew tip are updated to match.
- Numbers: jab / heavy / big damage 1 / 2 / 4 (was 1, and 2 on every third); hold for heavy 1.0 s, for big 2.0 s; daze none / 0.6 s / 1.1 s (bosses: none / none / 0.6 s; heavy creatures ×0.6); shove none / 3.5 / 7 (bosses ×0.25, heavy ×0.6); cooldown after a jab 0.55 s, after a heavy or big 0.4 s (was 0.48 s); walk speed while charging ×0.55; contact damage 1, reach your body + creature radius + 0.85, only while you're moving toward it (within 60°); spike prick 1; rancid chance 0.2 (salt ×0.5, jar ×2). Removed: ING `life` (80/65/45/70 s), the starting eye's 30 s head start.
- Code: `bonkDown`/`bonkUp`/`bonkTick`/`bonkTier` replace `bonk`; `bonkLand(tier)`; `BONK_HEAVY`, `BONK_FULL`; `vmState.charge`; the `#power` ring; `CONTACT_DMG` and the contact check in the player movement block; `spike()`, `TYPES.stump.spiky`, `TYPES.knight.spiky`, `buildStump`, `buildKnight`; `run.spikeTip`; `RANCID_CHANCE`, `rancidChance`, `gather` (replaces `checkSpoilage`, `lifeOf`, `freshness`); `ING.*.perishable`; `ITEMS.salt`, `ITEMS.jar`; `stockUp`; removed `drawChalk`, `chalkControls` and the start room's chalk keep-clear check in `buildRoom`; input handlers for V, right mouse and the touch BONK button (hold and release).

### 2026-10-26 · A guide book on the title screen
- A red spellbook sits in the bottom-right corner of the title screen. It bobs, a crack of its cover breathes open on its own, and it falls open with its first page glowing when you point at it. Click it to read the guide.
- The guide is a parchment two-page spread (one page at a time on a phone) with four chapters on tabs: Controls (keyboard or touch, depending on the device), Brewing (scraping, the pot, all eight ingredients, and the combos you've found so far), Creatures (one page per creature and boss) and Co-op.
- Creature pages fill in as you meet things: until then they show the creature as an ink silhouette marked "???". Each page has a picture of the creature, what it shrugs off or is weak to, and how to deal with it.
- Turn pages with the arrow keys, the dog-eared corners, or the chapter tabs. Esc closes it.
- Numbers: book 150px in the corner (112px on a phone); cover opens 2.15 rad on hover; spread up to 900px wide and 62% of the screen tall; 22 pages (2 controls, 4 brewing, 14 creatures, 2 co-op).
- Saves: creatures you've met are kept between runs (`slingcam.seen`). A creature counts as met once it's out in the open in front of you during a run, or when a boss's intro banner plays. The test lab and the title screen don't count.
- Code: new `guide` section (the book, the creature pictures, the pages, `SCREENS.guide`), `buildModel` (pulled out of `spawnEnemy` so the guide can build a creature on its own), `seen` and `meetCreature` in data, `bossIntro`, the enemy-label pass in the loop, `uiNav`.
- Roblox note: the creature pictures are taken from the real models; a viewport frame per page would do the same job.
