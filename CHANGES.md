# Changes

What's new in Brewtality, newest first.

Brewtality also has a Roblox version that copies this game exactly, and it reads this file to know what to copy. So every change to how the game plays, looks or sounds gets an entry here, in the same commit as the change. Write each entry like this:

```
### 2026-10-27 · Spitters aim ahead of you
- Spitter goo now leads a moving target instead of aiming where you stood.
- Numbers: goo speed 9 → 11; lead 0 → 0.35 s.
- Code: launchGoo, TYPES.spitter.
```

- **The first lines** are for players: what changed, in plain words.
- **Numbers** gives the old and new values, so they can be copied straight across.
- **Code** names the functions, tables or sections touched, so the change can be found without rereading the whole file.
- **Web only:** mark changes that only matter here, such as PeerJS co-op, the claude.ai page, the downloaded file, the test lab console, or this repo's tools. Write `Web only:` and the reason, so the Roblox side knows to skip it.
- **Fixes and refactors:** a bug fix is an entry too. A refactor with no visible effect gets one line.

### 2026-10-27 · The Cast-Iron Rim in the goblin shop
- **From floor 3, every goblin shop sells the Cast-Iron Rim** (the pot holds one more) on a fourth pedestal of its own, until you've bought it. It used to turn up only in secret shops behind the X brick, so most runs never reached a 4-slot pot. Secret shops still sell it early, and it still never comes out of a chest or the arcade's mystery prize. Beating bosses still takes the pot to 3 slots; the Rim is the only way to a 4th.
- Numbers: Rim 20 coins (endless floors add their usual +1 per floor past 5); offered by goblin shops on floor 3 and deeper. Goblin shops on floors 1–2 are unchanged.
- Code: ITEMS.rim gains shopFrom: 3; makeShop adds the Rim as an extra goblin slot after the random picks (it draws no randoms, so co-op partners' seeded shops still match).

### 2026-10-27 · Fix: walking straight back out skipped the boss; arcade fixes
- **Fix: you could skip any fight, bosses included.** Step into a room and straight back out inside half a second, and its creatures still spawned behind you during the fade. That left the room marked as mid-fight with nobody in it, so on your next visit the game counted it as won at once: doors open, the stairs open and the goblin shop out, with the boss still standing there. A visit after that, the room was empty. Now a room's fight only starts once its creatures are in, and nothing spawns if you're already on your way back out; the room just waits for you.
- **Cups**: the gnome now plays honest on your first round in every arcade (the round count never reset, so in a new run he could cheat on your very first game). With too few coins, it just says NEED 2 COINS; it no longer lifts a cup and shows a coin as if you'd played.
- **Gamepad at the counter**: at cups, the stick picks a cup (it bobs) and A lifts it, and Y calls him out; at the prize counter, the stick picks a prize and A buys it. The arrow keys and A/D/W/S do the same on a keyboard. Prizes now light up under the mouse too.
- **Skee-ball**: a game you've paid for and stepped away from now waits for you even if you leave the room, like the cabinets and Whack-a-Shroom already did.
- Numbers: cups' round is now this visit's play (1–3) instead of a count that never reset, so swaps are 7 + min(6, round + floor) and swap time 0.32 − 0.012 × (round + floor) s per visit.
- Code: enterRoom (r.fightActive cleared on entry; the delayed spawn skips if transit), arcade: release() shared by leave and endNow (being pulled out of a machine now lets go of held keys and the mallet), teardown keeps SK.st 'play', cupsStart (round from ARC_PLAYS.cups − den.cups, CP.dry for message-only results), cupsStep (no lift or coin when dry; the selected cup bobs), key() (cups and prize selection with arrows/WASD, space/enter to take), prizeSel, .arcPrize hover/.sel CSS, pollPad (Y sends 'c'), cupsHud (a pad hint).

### 2026-10-26 · Charged bonks, contact damage, spikes, and rancid on pickup
- **The bonk charges.** Tap for a jab: 1 damage, and it no longer dazes or shoves, so it can't interrupt an attack. Hold 1 s for a heavy bonk: 2 damage, dazes and shoves (bosses take the damage but aren't interrupted). - **The chalked controls on the first room's floor are gone** (the guide book covers them). Floor decor in that room is no longer kept clear of where the chalk was.
